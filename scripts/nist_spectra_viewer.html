Complete NIST records for this ion

Browse every imported line and energy level, including rows without a wavelength or energy. All stored columns, uncertainties, configurations, references, and source row numbers are shown. The CSV link exports the entire selected table. Extended line fields appear only for spectra whose direct NIST export matched the archived rows. A source audit verified all 15,310 indexed line, level, and isotope export files against their recorded hashes and ion counts. NIST's plasma-diagnostics query currently returns an error, so diagnostic links are not included. The derived source field check flags vacuum line rows whose stored wavenumber differs by more than 0.1% from the inverse observed wavelength or by over ten times the listed wavelength uncertainty; original NIST values remain unchanged. NIST explains that wavenumbers can have independent precision. NIST relative intensities are qualitative and do not share a scale across ions.

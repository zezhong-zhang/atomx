# Be-like ionization residual trend

> Historical report. Commands, paths, counts and status describe the recorded
> source snapshot; some campaign tools have since been retired. See the
> [current documentation](../../README.md) for supported usage.

The running corrected-v2 calculation exposes evaluated, unique-ground-level
ionization deficits along the four-electron sequence. These comparisons do
not require a configuration-centroid reinterpretation. The initial matched
ionization overview includes 68 qualifying comparisons; coverage changes
as calculation proceeds.

To test a correlation mechanism without fitting NIST, the script
`scripts/probe_be_like_configuration_mixing.py` optimizes the six-CSF space
generated by `1s2 2s2` and `1s2 2p2`, with the Dirac–Coulomb Hamiltonian.
All four calculations satisfy their orbital stationarity checks and have
J=0, even-parity ground states.

| Z | Four-electron ion charge | Corrected DHF − NIST IE (eV) | DC energy gained through mixing (eV) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 4 | 0 | −1.27808060 | 1.19226182 |
| 6 | 2 | −2.09308286 | 1.96987852 |
| 8 | 4 | −2.81027424 | 2.66356220 |
| 10 | 6 | −3.48947006 | 3.32355087 |

The systematic gain is close in scale to the growing DHF deficit, supporting
configuration mixing as a substantial missing contribution. This is an
inference from the controlled energy lowering, not a complete error budget.
Only the four-electron endpoint is correlated. The three-electron ion also
has correlation energy, and Breit, QED and recoil must be evaluated on the
correlated states before producing a combined prediction. Two configurations
are not an extrapolated active space, and stationarity does not certify a
global orbital minimum.

Machine-readable evidence: `output/nist_spectra/be_like_configuration_mixing.jsonl`.
The website shows the energy gains beside the original DHF deficits in a
grouped bar chart with these limitations. It does not replace production
DHF line or ionization energies with the diagnostic values.

The corrected ionization periodic table uses the actual corrected-v2 ledger,
retains the historical panels separately, and scores only evaluated unique
levels or complete evaluated configuration centroids. Missing predictions,
incomplete centroids and semiempirical/theoretical references remain unscored.
Reference-selection tests cover these exclusions explicitly.
